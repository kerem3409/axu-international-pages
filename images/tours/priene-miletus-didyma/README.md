# Priene, Miletus and Didyma photographs

Five original, real photographs are included locally. Their Wikimedia Commons source pages, creators, commercial-use licenses, original hashes and optimized-file hashes are recorded in `sources.json`. Commons confirms the stated Creative Commons licenses on the linked source pages.

- Carole Raddato: Temple of Apollo at Didyma, CC BY-SA 2.0.
- Ken Fairfax / Ken and Nyetta: Temple of Athena at Priene, CC BY 2.0.
- Ken and Nyetta: Ancient Theatre at Priene, CC BY 2.0.
- Bernard Gagnon: Ancient Theatre at Miletus and the Adyton of the Temple of Apollo at Didyma, CC BY-SA 3.0.

Images were resized to a maximum of 1920 pixels, saved as JPEG quality 86 and had metadata removed. The full image frame is retained for the gallery lightbox. The image area crops previews with the site’s existing styles.

Historical sources consulted: [Priene Örenyeri](https://muze.gov.tr/muze-detay?DistId=MRK&SectionId=PRI02), [Milet Ören Yeri](https://www.kulturportali.gov.tr/turkiye/aydin/gezilecekyer/milet-oren-yeri), and [Didim Apollon Tapınağı](https://muze.gov.tr/muze-detay?DistId=DDM&SectionId=DDM01).
